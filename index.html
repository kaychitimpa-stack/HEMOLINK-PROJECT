ABOUT HEMOLINK.

Transforming Blood Donation Through Smart Technology.

HemoLink is an innovative cloud-based Blood Bank Management System developed to improve the way blood is collecte, managed and distributed. The platform connects blood donors,blood banks and healthcare facilities through digital System.

By providing real-time blood inventory monitoring, smart donor matching and emergency blood Request management,HemoLink helps reduce delays, minimise blood shortages and support healthcare professionals in saving lives more efficiently.

BLOOD FACTS.

Know More About Blood Donation.

Understanding blood and blood donation can help make us make informed decisions and encourage more people to become regular donors.

Blood Is Essential.

Blood is needed everyday for surgeries,medical treatments, emergencies and patients recovering from serious illnesses.

One Donation Can Help.

A single blood donation can contribute to the care of multiple patients when the blood is seperated into different components.
